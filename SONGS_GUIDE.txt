HOW TO CREATE & MANAGE SONGS FROM THE ADMIN DASHBOARD
=====================================================

1. Starting the Process
-----------------------
From the main Admin Dashboard, navigate to the "Songs" section. 
- You will see the Songs Dashboard containing your church song library, preloaded master Christian hymns, and the "+ New Song" button at the top-right corner.
- Tapping "+ New Song" opens the Song Creation Modal with a clean, easy-to-use form.


2. Song Titles (English & Telugu)
---------------------------------
- Song Title — English (Required): 
  * The primary display name of the song (e.g., "Hosanna in the Highest" or "Nee Krupa Leni Kshaname").
- Song Title — Telugu (Optional but Recommended): 
  * The authentic Telugu title written in Telugu script (e.g., "నీ కృప లేని క్షణమే" or "హోసన్నా సర్వోన్నతమైన స్థలములలో").
  * Having both allows congregation members to search and read in either English or Telugu script!


3. Artist & Composer Attribution
--------------------------------
- Artist / Writer / Composer (Optional): 
  * Credit the original songwriter, worship ministry, or singer (e.g., "Bro. Yesanna", "Hemasagar", "Hillsong Worship", "Don Moen").
  * Appears right under the song title on the lyrics screen.


4. Musical Key (For Worship Teams & Musicians)
----------------------------------------------
- Musical Key: 
  * Specify the root musical key for church musicians and choir team (e.g., C, D, E, F, G, A, B, or minor keys like Em, Am, Dm).
  * Helps the church keyboardist, guitarist, and worship leader know the scale immediately when leading service!


5. Song Category (Ministry Tags)
--------------------------------
- Categories Selector: Select all ministry tags that apply to this song:
  * Stuthi Songs (Praise & Thanksgiving)
  * Aradhana Songs (Deep Worship & Adoration)
  * Offering Songs (Offertory & Giving)
  * Youth Songs (Fast beat, modern praise)
  * Christmas Songs (Advent & Nativity)
  * Easter Songs (Resurrection & Cross)
  * Gospel Songs (Evangelism & Salvation)
  * Marriage Songs (Wedding blessings)
  * Thanksgiving Songs (New Year & Harvest)
  * Special Songs (Choir specials & Solos)
  * Theme Songs (Church anniversary or yearly motto songs)
- Members can filter directly by these categories on Sunday morning or during mid-week prayer!


6. Complete Song Lyrics (The Heart of the Song)
----------------------------------------------
- Lyrics & Scripts (Required):
  * Type or paste the full verses, chorus, bridge, and ending.
  * Supports Telugu script, English lyrics, or transliterated Romanized Telugu (e.g., "Nee Krupa Leni Kshaname nenu brathakalene").
  * Clean Line Breaks: Format stanzas cleanly with line breaks between Verse 1, Chorus, and Verse 2 so members can easily sing along without squinting.


7. YouTube Link (Audio / Video Playback)
----------------------------------------
- YouTube Video URL / ID:
  * Paste the full YouTube link (e.g., https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=... or https://youtu.be/...) OR the 11-character video ID.
  * When members open the song lyrics in the app, they can tap the YouTube button to listen to the song and practice the tune!


8. Publish Status & Visibility
------------------------------
- Publish Status:
  1. Published: Goes live immediately on the Member App Songbook and search library.
  2. Draft: Saved privately in the Admin panel for review, completely hidden from members until finalized.


9. Final Actions & What Happens Next
------------------------------------
A. Save / Publish Song:
- Tapping "Save & Publish Song":
  * Instantly saves the song into your church's secure cloud database (`churches/{churchId}/worship_songs`).
  * Clears the in-memory cache so all members see the new song without delay.
  * THE MAGIC STEP: An automated Push Notification broadcast is dispatched to all church members:
    "🎵 New Song: [Title] — A new worship song '[Title]' has been posted under [Category]!"
  * A Success popup appears with your database sync receipt confirmation.

B. The Master Song Library:
- WeChristian comes preloaded with hundreds of beloved traditional and contemporary Telugu Christian songs and global hymns, so your church starts with a rich catalog on Day 1!

HOW MEMBERS USE THE SONGS FEATURE IN THE APP
============================================

1. The "Songs" Screen Tabs (SongsScreen.tsx)
--------------------------------------------
When members tap "Songs" in the app navigation, they have 4 dedicated browsing tabs:
- "Browse All": Access the complete songbook containing thousands of Telugu and English worship songs.
- "Church Own": Displays songs uniquely written, arranged, or uploaded by your specific local church.
- "Theme Songs": Highlights your church's special annual convention or theme hymns.
- "My Songbook": The member's personal favorites list! Members can tap the Star / Bookmark icon on any song to save it for Sunday choir practice or personal devotions.

2. Real-Time Instant Search
---------------------------
- Search by English Title (e.g., "Hosanna")
- Search by Telugu Title (e.g., "హోసన్నా")
- Search by Lyric words: Even if members don't know the title, typing a line from the chorus instantly finds the song!

3. The Lyric Reader Modal (SongDetailModal.tsx)
-----------------------------------------------
Tapping any song opens the full-screen Lyric Reader:
- Displays large, crisp typography optimized for singing in low-light church sanctuaries.
- Shows Song Title, Telugu script, Artist, and Musical Key.
- Bookmark Button: One-tap button to add or remove the song from personal favorites.
- WhatsApp Share Button: Allows members to easily share the song lyrics and YouTube link directly to their family or church prayer groups.
