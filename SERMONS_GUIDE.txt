1. OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
The Sermons feature in WeChristian enables church administrators and pastors
to upload, organize, publish, and broadcast video and audio messages to their
entire congregation.

When a sermon is created and published:
- It appears instantly in the Member App under the "Sermons" section.
- It is categorized automatically into ministry series (e.g., Sunday Service,
  Bible Study, Youth Meeting, Special Messages).
- Members can stream YouTube video sermons or listen to MP3 sermon audio.
- An automatic Push Notification broadcast is sent to all church members' phones.
- Sermons can be saved as private 'Drafts' while being prepared and published
  only when ready.

2. PART 1: THE "SERMONS" TAB (DASHBOARD & LIST VIEW)
================================================================================

[ ACCESSING THE SERMONS DASHBOARD ]
1. Log in to the Admin Dashboard.
2. Select the "Sermons" tab from the admin navigation menu.

[ TOP HERO HEADER BAR ]
* "< Back" Button:
  - Returns you to the main Admin Dashboard view.
* "Sermons" Title:
  - Header showing total count of sermons and active series.
  - Subtitle: e.g., "18 total · 5 series".
* "+ New" Button (Top-Right):
  - Opens the "New Sermon" form with fresh, empty input fields ready for a new
    entry.

[ THREE CLICKABLE STAT FILTER CARDS ]
At the top of the Sermons list are three quick-filter summary cards:

1. "Published" Card (Forest Green):
   - Displays the total number of live sermons currently visible to members.
   - CLICK ACTION: Instantly filters the list to show only published sermons.

2. "Drafts" Card (Gold / Ochre):
   - Displays the number of private, unreleased draft sermons.
   - CLICK ACTION: Instantly filters the list to display all drafts so you can
     edit or publish them.

3. "Series" Card (Royal Navy):
   - Displays the count of distinct sermon categories/series in your church.
   - CLICK ACTION: Automatically organizes the list into grouped sections under
     each Series Name.

[ HORIZONTAL CATEGORY FILTER CHIPS ]
Below the stats bar is a scrollable row of category pills:
* "All (Count)"
* "Sunday Service (Count)"
* "Bible Study (Count)"
* "Youth Meeting (Count)"
* "Special Messages (Count)"
* "Women's Fasting Prayer (Count)"
* "Testimonies (Count)"
- Tapping any chip filters the list in real-time.

[ INDIVIDUAL SERMON LIST CARDS ]
Each sermon card contains:
* Play Thumbnail Button (Left):
  - Tapping this button plays the sermon video directly (via YouTube) or audio
    stream.
* Sermon Titles:
  - English Title (bold).
  - Native Telugu Title (if provided).
* Metadata Line:
  - Pastor Name · Sermon Date (YYYY-MM-DD) · Duration (e.g., "Pastor John ·
    2026-09-14 · 45 min").
* Tag Badges:
  - Series / Category pill badges.
  - "📺 YouTube" badge if linked to video.
  - "🎙️ Audio" badge if MP3 audio is attached.
  - Status Pill: Green "Published" or Gold "Draft".
* Action Buttons (Right):
  - "Edit" (Navy): Loads all sermon details into the Editor.
  - "Del" (Red): Opens the secure delete confirmation modal.

[ DELETE CONFIRMATION & NOTIFICATION ]
- Tapping "Del" triggers a safety confirmation modal:
  "Delete Sermon? Are you sure you want to delete '[Title]'? This action
   cannot be undone."
- Clicking "Delete" permanently removes the sermon and displays a confirmation
  toast.

3. PART 2: STEP-BY-STEP: HOW TO CREATE A NEW SERMON
================================================================================

To create a new sermon, tap "+ New" from the Sermons list or navigate to the
"New Sermon" tab in the Admin menu.

Follow these steps to fill out the form:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: ENTER SERMON INFO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Title — English * (Mandatory):
   - Enter the main sermon title in English.
   - Example: "Walking in Faith Through Trials" or "The Power of Persistent Prayer"

2. Title — Telugu (Optional):
   - Enter the title in native Telugu font.
   - Example: "శ్రమలలో విశ్వాస ప్రయాణం"

3. Pastor Name * (Mandatory):
   - Enter the name of the pastor or guest speaker who preached the sermon.
   - Example: "Pastor Daniel Raju" or "Evangelist John Wesley"

4. Sermon Date (Mandatory):
   - By default, it is set to today's date.
   - Tap the calendar input to open the interactive Date Picker modal.
   - Select the exact date the sermon was preached or scheduled.

5. Duration (Optional):
   - Enter approximate sermon length.
   - Examples: "42 min", "1 hr 10 min", "55 mins"

6. Scripture Reference (Optional):
   - Enter the primary biblical passage for the message.
   - Examples: "James 1:2-4", "Romans 8:28", "Isaiah 40:29-31"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: SELECT SERMON CATEGORIES (CHIPS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Select one or more categories that describe the message. Members will see the
sermon organized under these categories in their app:

Available categories:
- [ ] Sunday Service          (ఆదివారపు ఆరాధన)
- [ ] Bible Study             (బైబిల్ అధ్యయనం)
- [ ] Youth Meeting           (యవ్వనస్థుల కూడిక)
- [ ] Women's Fasting Prayer  (స్త్రీల ఉపవాస ప్రార్థన)
- [ ] Second Saturday Prayer  (రెండవ శనివారపు ప్రార్థన)
- [ ] All-Night Prayer        (రాత్రంతా ప్రార్థన)
- [ ] Revival Meeting         (ఉజ్జీవ కూడికలు)
- [ ] Special Messages        (ప్రత్యేక వర్తమానాలు)
- [ ] Shorts                  (చిన్న వీడియోలు / రీల్స్)
- [ ] Testimonies             (సాక్ష్యాలు)

* Note: You can tap multiple categories. A selected badge shows a golden
  checkmark icon.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: ATTACH MEDIA & DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. YouTube Video URL (Recommended):
   - Paste the complete YouTube link or 11-character video ID.
   - Supported formats:
     * https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ
     * https://youtu.be/dQw4w9WgXcQ
     * https://www.youtube.com/shorts/dQw4w9WgXcQ
     * https://www.youtube.com/live/dQw4w9WgXcQ
     * Plain 11-character ID: "dQw4w9WgXcQ"
   - Benefit: Automatic YouTube thumbnail detection and built-in full-screen
     streaming inside the Member App!

2. Custom Thumbnail (Optional):
   - Upload a custom 16:9 graphic if you do not want to use the default YouTube
     thumbnail (auto-fetched from YouTube by default).

4. Description / Sermon Notes (Optional):
   - Enter a brief summary, sermon outline, key takeaways, or prayer points.
   - This will be displayed directly underneath the video in the member app.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: CONFIGURE PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under the "Push Notification" section:
* Toggle "Notify members when published":
  - When enabled (Green Switch), a broadcast push notification is sent to all
    congregants' smartphones as soon as the sermon is published.
* Live Lock-Screen Notification Preview:
  - The editor shows an exact live preview of the lock-screen notification:
    Header: [Church Emblem] Your Church · Now
    Title:  New Sermon 🎙️
    Body:   [Sermon Title] · [Pastor Name] · Watch now

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: CHOOSE PUBLISH STATUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tap the "Publish Status" dropdown to select one of three states:

1. "Publish now — visible to all members":
   - The sermon goes live immediately in the Member App.
   - Push notification is queued and delivered.

2. "Draft — not visible to members yet":
   - The sermon is safely stored in the database.
   - Hidden from members until you choose to publish it later.

3. "Schedule for a specific date & time":
   - Queued for publication on the specified sermon date.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6: SAVE & PUBLISH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At the bottom of the screen, you have two primary buttons plus a floating save button:

* "Save as Draft" (Button with File icon):
  - Saves all entered details as a private draft without notifying members.

* "Publish" (Emerald Green Button with Checkmark):
  - Saves all details, publishes the sermon live to the church, and triggers the
    push notification.

* Floating Save Button (FAB at bottom-right):
  - Convenient floating button to publish with one tap from anywhere on the screen.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7: SUCCESS CONFIRMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Upon saving:
- A celebratory "Success!" modal appears with a green checkmark:
  "Your sermon metadata has been saved successfully."
- Tap "Done" to automatically return to the Sermons List, where your new sermon
  is displayed at the top!

4. PART 3: SERMON MEDIA & YOUTUBE STREAMING
================================================================================

1. YOUTUBE VIDEO STREAMING:
   - When a YouTube link is supplied, the app's integrated YouTube iframe player
     loads the video inside the app.
   - Supports HD playback, forward/rewind, pause/play, and fullscreen mode.
   - Members never have to leave the church app to watch Sunday messages.

2. EMBEDDED SHARING:
   - In the video player screen, members have a "Share" button.
   - Clicking "Share" generates a ready-to-share message for WhatsApp and SMS:
     "Watch this powerful teaching: '[Title]' - https://youtu.be/[ID]"

3. PLAYLIST / NEXT SERMONS CAROUSEL:
   - Below the active video player, members can see upcoming and recent sermons
     in the playlist to continue listening seamlessly.

5. PART 4: CATEGORIES, SERIES & FILTERING SYSTEM
================================================================================

Why Categories Matter:
- A typical church has distinct types of services during the week:
  * Sunday Morning & Evening Services
  * Mid-week Bible Studies
  * Youth Fellowships
  * Women's Fasting Prayers & All-Night Vigils
- By assigning categories in the editor:
  1. The Admin can view stats per series.
  2. The Admin can group sermons by series using the "Series" stat card.
  3. Members can browse by category chips in their app with distinctive colors:
     - Bible Study: Deep Navy (#1a2d5a)
     - Sunday Service: Ocean Blue (#0369a1)
     - Youth Meeting: Forest Green (#15803d)
     - Women's Fasting Prayer: Berry Pink (#be185d)
     - All-Night Prayer: Royal Blue (#1d4ed8)
     - Revival Meeting: Amber Bronze (#b45309)
     - Special Messages: Crimson Red (#c0392b)

================================================================================
6. PART 5: PUSH NOTIFICATIONS & LIVE MEMBER APP SYNC
================================================================================

When you click "Publish":
1. The sermon document is written to `churches/{churchId}/sermons`.
2. The broadcast record is created in `churches/{churchId}/broadcasts`:
   - Title: "🎧 New Sermon: [Title]"
   - Content: "New sermon '[Title]' by [Pastor Name] is now available. Watch/listen now!"
   - Type: 'sermon'
   - Timestamp: Server timestamp
3. Any member with the app installed receives the push notification in real time.
4. Tapping the notification takes the member directly to the sermon!

================================================================================
7. PART 6: HOW MEMBERS VIEW & WATCH SERMONS
================================================================================

In the WeChristian Member App:
1. Members tap "Sermons" from the main navigation or home screen shortcut.
2. They see:
   - Category filter pills at the top.
   - Collapsible/expandable category accordion sections.
   - Video thumbnail, title, speaker name, duration, and date.
3. Tapping a sermon opens `SermonVideoScreen`:
   - Full 16:9 YouTube video playback.
   - Interactive share button for WhatsApp.
   - Sermon notes and description.
   - Up-next sermons list.

8. BONUS: AI SERMON ASSISTANT INTEGRATION
================================================================================

Need sermon inspiration or an outline before preaching?
WeChristian includes a built-in "AI Sermon Assistant" in the Admin menu!

Features:
- Select from Life Events:
  * Birthday, Wedding, Baptism, Healing, Funeral, Family Function, etc.
- Select from Spiritual Topics:
  * Joy, Peace, Faith, Hope, Love, Wisdom, Prayer, Holiness, Grace, etc.
- Choose Language: English or Telugu (తెలుగు).
- Generates:
  * Complete biblical sermon titles
  * Scripture readings & verse references
  * 3-Point homiletical sermon outlines with illustrations
  * Application and closing prayers
- You can copy the generated sermon directly into the Sermon Description
  field in the Sermon Editor!
