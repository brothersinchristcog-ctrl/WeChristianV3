HOW TO MANAGE & ADD MEMBERS FROM THE ADMIN DASHBOARD
===================================================

1. Starting the Process
-----------------------
From the main Admin Dashboard, tap on the "Members" section.
- You will see the Members Dashboard displaying your total church membership count, active members, and inactive members.
- At the top right corner, tap the Options menu (⋮) to reveal the Member Options:
  * "Add From Contacts": Directly accesses your phone's contact list to select and bulk-add multiple church members into your church database at once.
  * "Add Member": Opens the manual registration form to add an individual member with full profile details.


2. Adding a New Member (Member Registration Form)
------------------------------------------------
When you tap "+ Add Member", the modal opens with the following fields:

A. Full Name (Required):
   - The member's full legal or church name (e.g., "Daniel Raju" or "Mary Margaret").

B. Phone Number (Required):
   - 10-digit mobile phone number (e.g., "9876543210").
   - This serves as their primary login credential and church communication number.

C. City / Village / Area (Recommended):
   - The member's residential village, town, or neighborhood (e.g., "Guntur", "Kakinada", "Old Town", "Sector 4").
   - This allows pastors to filter members by area for cottage prayer meetings or pastoral home visits!

D. User Role / Access Level:
   - "Member": Standard congregation member with access to sermons, songs, prayers, and events.
   - "Admin": Co-pastor or church media coordinator with full admin dashboard privileges.


3. Spiritual & Family Milestones (Celebration Dates)
---------------------------------------------------
These dates feed directly into the WeChristian automated Church Celebrations module:

- Date of Birth (DOB):
  * Tap the calendar icon to pick their birthday.
  * On their birthday, the app automatically greets them, alerts the pastor to pray, and lets members wish them in the celebrations chat!

- Baptism Date:
  * Record the exact spiritual milestone date when the member was baptized in water.

- Wedding Anniversary Date:
  * For married members, record their marriage date to celebrate their anniversary each year with pastoral blessings.


4. Search, Filtering & Village Grouping
---------------------------------------
A. Live Search Bar:
   - Search instantly by member name, phone number, village/city, or email.

B. Status Filter Pills:
   - "All": Displays complete church directory.
   - "Active": Shows members who regularly use the church app and attend services.
   - "Inactive": Highlights members who haven't opened the app recently so pastoral care teams can follow up.

C. Village / Area Dropdown:
   - Filter your entire congregation by their specific village or neighborhood. Perfect for organizing localized fellowship groups and area prayer cells!


5. Member Card Actions & Household Family Tree
----------------------------------------------
Each member card in the dashboard provides rich, one-tap pastoral tools:

- Direct Contact Buttons:
  * Phone Icon: Initiates an instant phone call (`tel:...`) directly from the dashboard.
  * WhatsApp Icon: Opens WhatsApp to send an encouraging message or prayer scripture directly to their chat.

- Expandable Details:
  * Tap any member card to reveal their full profile: Birthday, Baptism date, Anniversary date, Village, and last app login time.

- Household & Family Linking:
  * The system automatically links members belonging to the same family or household, allowing pastors to see parents, spouses, and children together!


6. Role Management (Promoting & Demoting Admins)
-----------------------------------------------
Pastors can delegate administrative duties to associate pastors, youth leaders, or media teams:
- Promote to Admin: Tap the 3 dots on a member card and select "Promote to Admin". They immediately receive access to the Admin Dashboard.
- Remove Admin Role: Select "Remove Admin Role" to safely revert their permissions back to a regular member at any time.


7. Adding Members via "Add From Contacts" (InviteMembersModal.tsx)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Tapping "Add From Contacts" from the Member Options menu allows you to bulk-import church members straight from your phonebook without typing each name and number manually:

A. Phonebook Permission & Loading:
   - The app asks for contacts permission once, then instantly loads all contacts from your device with valid phone numbers.

B. Search & Multi-Selection:
   - Search: Type any name or phone number in the search bar to locate specific believers.
   - Select All / Deselect All: One-tap buttons to quickly select all filtered contacts.
   - Selection Counter: Shows the live count of selected contacts (e.g., "12 Selected").

C. Automatic Database Import & Duplicate Protection:
   - When you proceed, WeChristian automatically adds every selected contact directly into your church's database in Firestore.
   - Duplicate Check: Compares phone numbers against existing members so no duplicate records are created.
   - Real-Time Sync: The dashboard's Total Members count updates immediately with the newly added members.

D. Sending Welcome Message (SMS / WhatsApp):
   - Send SMS: Sends a personalized SMS message with the church app link and church code.
   - WhatsApp: Opens WhatsApp with a pre-formatted message welcoming them to the church app with login instructions using their registered mobile number.

HOW MEMBERS USE THE MEMBERS DIRECTORY IN THE APP (MembersScreen.tsx)
====================================================================

1. Family Profile & Household Tree
----------------------------------
When a member opens the "Members" or "Family" section in the app:
- They can view their primary profile and manage their immediate household.
- Tap "+ Add Family Member" to register their spouse, children, or elderly parents.

2. Relationship Tagging
-----------------------
When adding family members, members can select their exact relationship:
- Husband, Wife, Father, Mother, Son, Daughter, Brother, Sister, Grandfather, Grandmother, etc.

3. Pastoral Care Connection
---------------------------
- Congregation members can quickly view church elders, pastoral staff, and emergency prayer team contacts to request prayer or pastoral visitation when needed.
